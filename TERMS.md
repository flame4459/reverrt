Revert is a personal, non-commercial project provided as-is, without warranty.

## Riding

Revert is a planning and navigation aid, not a substitute for your attention,
road signs, traffic law or your own judgement. Route suggestions are based on
OpenStreetMap and Apple Maps data that can be incomplete, out of date or wrong
about surface, access and turn restrictions. Ride within your limits and obey
local law.

Do not operate the phone while riding. Set the route before you set off, use
mounted controls, and let the voice prompts do the talking.

## Data and availability

Map, weather and routing sources are third-party and can be unavailable, rate
limited or inaccurate. Downloaded regions can be evicted by iOS. Keep an offline
copy of anything you depend on.
